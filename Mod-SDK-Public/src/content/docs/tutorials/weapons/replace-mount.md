---
title: "Replace a mounted weapon"
description: "Assign a weapon to a unit's mount."
---

Clone the unit first if you want an additional variant.

## Steps

1. Select the unit in **Showroom** and open **Mounts**.
2. Find the turret or mount to change.
3. Open its weapon selector, search for the replacement, and check compatibility.
4. Select the weapon and finish any pending assignment controls.
5. Check the mount's ammunition or salvo-pool count.
6. Inspect the model, turret orientation, and payload in the preview.
7. Repack and use the unit in game.
