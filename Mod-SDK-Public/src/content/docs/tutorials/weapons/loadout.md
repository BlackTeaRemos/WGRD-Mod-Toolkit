## Steps

1. Select the unit in **Showroom** and open its loadout controls.
2. Choose the station or slot to change.
3. Open its picker and select a payload or carriage from the catalogue.
4. Inspect the selected relationship and model preview.
5. Complete any **Apply** or **Save** action, then revisit the unit.
6. Repack and check the loadout in game.

Use [Connectors](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/weapons/connectors/) for permanent pylon geometry.

Apply or discard a pending draft before changing the active mod.
