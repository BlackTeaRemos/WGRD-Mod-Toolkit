---
title: "Edit a weapon"
description: "Change weapon or ammunition stats."
---

## Steps

1. Select a unit in **Showroom**, open **Mounts**, and follow its weapon into **Weapons**.
2. Use **Clone** if you need an independent weapon variant.
3. Open **Stats**, find the field, and edit it using the displayed units.
4. Finish saving and return to the weapon to check the value.
5. If you made a variant, assign it to the unit's mount.
6. Repack and compare the weapon in game.

Editing a shared weapon affects its other users.
