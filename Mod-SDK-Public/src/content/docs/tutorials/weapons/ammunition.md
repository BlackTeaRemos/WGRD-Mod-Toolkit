---
title: "Inspect ammunition and carried supply"
description: "Edit carried rounds or ammunition behavior."
---

## Steps

1. Open the unit's **Mounts** inspector and find its weapon and ammunition.
2. Choose the control for the change you want:

| Change | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Rounds carried by this unit | The carrier or mount's count |
| Projectile behavior | The ammunition's fields in **Weapons** |
| Assigned weapon | The mount's weapon selector |

3. Open **Stats** or **NDF** to inspect the relevant definition.
4. Edit the value, finish saving, and return to the entry.
5. Repack and compare the same carrier and weapon in game.
