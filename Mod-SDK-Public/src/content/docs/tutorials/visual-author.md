---
title: "Give a mod its own appearance"
description: "Replace icons, textures, flags, and connectors."
---

Select an editable mod and prepare the replacement image or FBX.

Choose the task:

- [Unit icon](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/visuals/unit-icon/): the unit's catalogue picture.
- [Deck artwork](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/artwork/): a specialization or era image.
- [Flags](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/coalition/): country or coalition artwork.
- [Model texture](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/visuals/replace-texture/): a model surface.
- [Connector](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/weapons/connectors/): permanent pylon geometry.
- [FBX export](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/visuals/export-model/): a model for an external editor.
