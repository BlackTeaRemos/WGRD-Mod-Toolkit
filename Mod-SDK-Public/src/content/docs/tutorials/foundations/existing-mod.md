---
title: "Inspect an imported project"
description: "Open a mod and find its editable changes."
---

## Steps

1. Open the editable mod, or use **Unpack** for installed packs. Read the replacement confirmation if source already exists.
2. In **Showroom**, select a changed unit and inspect its identifier, name, and stats.
3. Open one of its weapons or images and check that the asset is available.
4. In **Decks**, inspect a related entry's membership and rules.
5. Read broken-definition diagnostics in the unit or weapon inspector.
