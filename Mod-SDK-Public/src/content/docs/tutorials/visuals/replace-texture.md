---
title: "Replace a model texture"
description: "Import a texture through the unit's material."
---

## Steps

1. Select the unit in **Showroom** and open **Material**.
2. Choose the model part and its texture.
3. Export the current texture if you need its layout as a reference.
4. Import the replacement using a format offered by the picker.
5. Check the texture on the model. Complete **Apply** if you also edited material values.
6. Reopen the unit, check the relevant LODs, and repack.

Keep the texture's layout and transparency. If it looks stretched or appears on the wrong surface, check the material and UV mapping.
