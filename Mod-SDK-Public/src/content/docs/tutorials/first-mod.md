---
title: "From installation to a first mod"
description: "Install the Toolkit and make a small mod."
---

You need Windows, Wargame: Red Dragon, more than 100 GB of free workspace space, and WRG-Patcher. See [installation requirements](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/start/installation/).

## Steps

1. Install the Toolkit and [prepare a workspace and mod](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/start/controls/).
2. [Create your first mod change](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/foundations/first-mod/).
3. [Reopen the project](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/foundations/reopen-project/) and check that the edit was saved.
4. Use [Repack](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/start/controls/).
5. Enable the mod in game and find the changed unit or deck entry.
