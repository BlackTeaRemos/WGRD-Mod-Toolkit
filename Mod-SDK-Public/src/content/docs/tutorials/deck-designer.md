---
title: "Design a deck selection"
description: "Create deck types, coalitions, and their rules."
---

Select an editable mod and open **Decks**.

Choose what to create:

- [Specialization](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/specialization/): deck-type bonuses.
- [Era category](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/era/): a production-year limit.
- [Coalition](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/coalition/): a group of countries.

## Steps

1. Set the entry's **General** options and selector visibility.
2. Assign a **Deck-code slot** for a new specialization or era.
3. Set [combinations and availability](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/availability/).
4. Check a [unit's eligibility](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/units/change-membership/).
5. Add [bonus artwork](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/decks/artwork/) or flags.
6. Repack and open the deck selection in game.
