---
title: "Build a unit variant"
description: "Clone a unit and change its gameplay."
---

Select an editable mod and a source unit similar to the variant you want.

## Steps

1. [Clone the unit](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/units/clone-unit/).
2. [Set its identifier and displayed name](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/units/rename-unit/).
3. [Change its gameplay values](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/units/rebalance/).
4. [Edit a weapon](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/weapons/edit-weapon/) or [replace a mounted weapon](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/weapons/replace-mount/) if needed.
5. Set its [deck eligibility](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/tutorials/units/change-membership/).
6. Repack and find the variant in the intended game deck.
