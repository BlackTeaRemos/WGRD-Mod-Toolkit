Decks edits the entries used by deck selection. Select an entry to open its controls.

For individual fields and calculations, see [deck parameters](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/parameters/decks/).

## Entries

- **Baseline** sets starting activation points, costs, and slots.
- **Specialization** sets bonuses for a deck type.
- **Era category** sets a production-year limit and bonuses.
- **Country** and **Coalition** hold political rules and flags.
- **Alliance** lists the countries offered by a side.
- **Emoji** holds tagged interface images.

## Inspector tabs

1. Use **Costs**, **Slots**, **Veterancy**, and **Availability** for their respective bonuses.
2. Use **Combinations** for permitted country, coalition, specialization, and era selections.
3. Use **Members** for coalition or alliance countries.
4. Use **Translations** for displayed names.
5. Use **Appearance** for specialization and era bonus images.
6. Use **Flags** for country, coalition, and alliance flags.

**Listed in deck selector** controls visibility. **Deck-code slot** registers a specialization or era for deck codes.

The inspector identifies unrestricted entries whose bonuses or year limits are ignored.
