Showrom is your main workspace tab.

For individual fields, use [unit parameters](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/parameters/units/), [movement and sensors](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/parameters/movement-sensors/), and [models, materials and animation](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/parameters/visuals/). [Values and ownership](/WGRD-Mod-Toolkit/parameters/values-and-ownership/) explains raw versus displayed units and compound edits.

## Per-unit tabs

1. Use **Stats** as generalized editing list
2. Use **Mounts** for attached weapons
3. Use **Material** for the unit appearance
4. Use **Translations** for displayed names
5. Use **Objects** or **NDF** for deeper diagnostics

## Identity and displayed text

A unit identifier is the stable name of the authored definition. A displayed name is text shown to a player and can have translations. Decide which of those you mean before editing **General name** or opening **Translations**.

Only cloned units can have their stable name changed.

## Broken definitions

Under different unpredictable conditions, broken definitions might apprear. Sometimes that's my fault. Report the problem, or, if you don't care enough, go into the mods folder into the workspace, find broken defintion, and delete it.
